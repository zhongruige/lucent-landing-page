Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your information

Lucent stores the budgets, categories, transactions, notes, payment methods, recurring rules, savings goals and receipts you choose to enter. Guest mode uses identifiers stored locally and in your private iCloud account. If you sign in with Apple or use email authentication, account information can include your user identifier, name and email address, including an Apple private relay address. Profile names help invited household members recognize each other.

Storage and household sharing

Your private app data can sync between your own devices using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services. Signed-in household data is stored and synchronized through Supabase. Invited household members can access financial records according to the app's sharing features. Receipt files are not synchronized to other household members through Supabase in this release.

Receipts and on-device recognition

Receipt text recognition uses Apple Vision on your device. Recognized text suggests transaction details for you to review and is not retained as a separate transcript. Receipt images you attach remain part of your stored transaction data.

Lucent Plus purchases

Apple processes Lucent Plus payments and supplies purchase and entitlement information used to unlock paid features. Lucent does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple's Family Sharing of purchases is separate from membership in a Lucent household.

Access and deletion

You can view your entered records, export supported records to CSV, and delete individual items within the app. Deleting shared records can affect what household members see.

You can permanently delete your account in Settings → Profile → Delete Account. This removes your profile, personal notes and personal goals. Households with no remaining members and their records are deleted. Shared financial records stay with remaining household members, and another member takes over their management and household ownership when needed. Lucent data on the device used for deletion is removed, with removal from your private iCloud data synchronized when iCloud is available. Copies exported by you or other members are not removed. Deleting your Lucent account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

For questions about your data, contact hello@gregbell.dev.

Service providers and analytics

Lucent uses Supabase for signed-in storage and synchronization, Apple services for private iCloud synchronization and purchases, and ExchangeRate-API for currency exchange rates. Exchange-rate requests use currency codes. Service providers necessarily receive technical connection information such as IP addresses. Lucent has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not sell your data.

Location and children

Lucent does not request device location access. The app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact hello@gregbell.dev.

Contact and changes

If these practices materially change, we will update this page and provide notice in the app or on the website. For privacy questions, contact hello@gregbell.dev.
